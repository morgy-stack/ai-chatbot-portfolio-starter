# Northstar Online Store — Sample FAQ

This is fictional sample content for a portfolio demonstration.

## Products
Northstar Online Store sells reusable water bottles, travel mugs, and insulated lunch bags.

## Opening hours
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM US Eastern Time.

## Shipping
Orders are normally prepared within 2 business days. Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

## Returns
Unused items can be requested for return within 30 days of delivery. Customers should contact support before sending an item back. Return shipping fees may apply.

## Order changes
Customers should contact support as soon as possible to request an order change. Changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has been dispatched.

## Contact
For order-specific assistance, customers should use the contact form on the store website. This demo does not process orders or access customer accounts.
